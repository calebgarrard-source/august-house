Pottery made in this room

Clay workshops at our table

Questions we hear at the counter

Do you still serve ice cream?

No. Our last scoop was June 27, 2026, the day we left the square.

Do you ship?

Yes. Call or message us and we'll work out shipping.

What if I can't make my workshop?

Seats are non-refundable. In an emergency we can move what you paid to a gift card, good for anything in the shop or another workshop.

Can I bring my children to a workshop?

Yes, at any age. We ask an adult to stay and help until they can work on their own.
